Features

Everything you need, nothing that phones home.

Cadence turns your voice into text in any app, on your Mac, in two languages. Here is all of it, and how it stacks up.

What it does

  • On your Mac, start to finish

    Speech becomes text on your Mac's own chip, using OpenAI's open Whisper model. No server, no account, and no networking code at all. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still works.

  • Two languages, two keys

    Right Option for one language, Left Option for the other. Switching costs a finger, not a menu, and no time spent guessing the language.

  • Your words, spelled right

    A personal dictionary teaches it the names and terms you use most.

  • History, encrypted

    Past dictations are kept in an encrypted database on your Mac, searchable, with copy and re-insert. Saving audio is off unless you turn it on.

  • See the time you save

    A dashboard with time saved, words, speaking pace, your streak and where you dictate most.

  • Honest about every insert

    Cadence checks that your text actually landed and never claims success when it didn't.

Controls

Everything is one key away.

Do thisTo
Hold Right ⌥, speak, releaseDictate in your first language. The text lands where your cursor is.
Hold Left ⌥, speak, releaseDictate in your second language (turn it on in the Cadence menu).
Tap twice, or tap then holdGo hands-free for long dictation. Let go and keep talking. The pause between presses can be up to about 0.7 seconds (it follows your Mac's double-click speed).
Tap onceFinish hands-free and insert. It never presses Return for you.
EscCancel the dictation in progress.
Ctrl⌥⌘ZTake back the last dictation.
Ctrl⌥⌘VTurn verbatim mode on or off: exactly what was heard, no cleanup.

Option shortcuts like ⌥E for an accent keep working: typing while you hold Option cancels the dictation instead of transcribing silence.
