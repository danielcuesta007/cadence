Our promises

Last updated 2 November 2026

Cadence is free and open source, and it stays that way. One day there may also be a paid version for people who want extra convenience. This page says, in public, what that will never take away.

Free forever

These stay in the free app, with every fix to them, for good:

  • Dictation in any app.
  • On-device speech recognition.
  • Both language keys: two languages, one key each.
  • Encrypted history.
  • Noisy Room Mode.
  • Anything already shipped for free.

Never paywalled

  • Privacy and offline use. The free app gets no accounts and no tracking.
  • If a paid version ever checks a licence, the check works offline.
  • Paid extras would live in a separate add-on. The free app never needs it to build or run.
  • Building Cadence yourself from the source stays free.

Every version already released stays MIT licensed, whatever happens next.

What’s next

Checkpoints, in order. Each waits for the one before it.

  1. In progress

    Stay public through TreeHacks, February 2027. The code stays open on GitHub under MIT.

  2. Next

    Get the basics right before charging anything. Settle the name, confirm ownership with Notre Dame, form an LLC, and pick a payment provider that handles sales tax.

  3. Later

    Join Apple’s developer program and notarize Cadence. That ends the Open Anyway step for the paid build.

  4. Later

    Add plug-in points for paid extras. The free app keeps building and passing its tests with none installed.

  5. Later

    Ship the paid build: signed and notarized by Apple, with automatic updates and support. The free download and Homebrew install keep working, unchanged.

Ideas for paid extras, not promises: sync across your Macs, team and admin controls, and transcribing files and meetings.

Contributing

Before the first outside pull request is accepted, Cadence will add a contributor agreement, so it is clear who may ship the work. Until then, issues on GitHub are the best way to help.
