About the creator

Built by DC Labs, a Daniel J. Cuesta company.

Daniel is a student founder who runs a lot at once: Addisuna Coffee, a coffee company where every bag funds a student's learning in Ethiopia, and Wednesdays of Wisdom, a weekly letter. He talks faster than he types, in English and Spanish, and wanted dictation that kept his words on his own machine. So he built it. DC Labs is the studio behind the tools he makes.
