Versions

Version history

Every release of Cadence, newest first. To update, run the install line again; your history and settings stay.

  1. 0.1.7

    Not released yetIn testing

    Background-noise captions removed more carefully

    • When Cadence removes a background-noise caption such as [Music], it no longer changes the spacing or line breaks in the rest of your text.
    • A number that starts with a decimal point, like .5, keeps its point when a caption comes right before it.

  2. 0.1.6

    November 2, 2026Latest

    Clearer about your microphone in loud rooms

    • Sound captions such as [indistinct chatter] or (music) are no longer typed into your text when a room is loud.
    • The Cadence menu names the microphone you are really using, and says when it is a lower-quality headset mic.
    • With earbuds connected, Cadence listens through their mic, which is closer to your mouth in a loud room. Turn on Always Use Built-in Microphone to keep your Mac's mic instead.
    • New Noisy Room Mode in Dictation Settings: fewer mistakes when other people are talking nearby, at the cost of slower results. Off by default.

    Release page and downloadSHA-256 7e1933c358aa05451e77b2749e5bb96cc34d8ddd0aa4232c83580ff5546d16d3

  3. 0.1.5

    November 1, 2026

    Forgiving hands-free and a smoother install

    • Go hands-free by tapping Option twice, or tapping once and then holding. One tap finishes.
    • The pause allowed between taps follows your Mac's double-click speed, with extra room on top.
    • The pill says “tap again for hands-free” after the first tap, so you can see it registered.
    • Dictation Settings shows the microphone and voice filtering actually in use.
    • The app menu shows the installed version.
    • Homebrew installs without an admin password or a security warning.
