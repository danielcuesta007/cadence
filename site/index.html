Listening · EN

A browser demo: talk and the note fills in. The browser only hears how loud you are, so it types a sample sentence; the app types your real words. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent, and Stop gives the microphone back.

How it works

Hold, speak, release.

  1. Hold

    Hold Right ⌥ in any app. A small pill appears while you speak.

  2. Speak

    A quick draft shows as you talk. A second, more careful pass makes the final text.

  3. Release

    Let go and the text lands where your cursor is. Ctrl⌥⌘Z takes it back.

Talking for a while? Tap Right ⌥ twice, or tap it once and then hold, to go hands-free. Let go and keep talking; one tap finishes. All the controls

Why Cadence

Private, fast and free.

  • Stays on your Mac

    Speech becomes text on your Mac's own chip. No server, no account, no networking code. It works with Wi-Fi off.

  • Two languages, two keys

    Right Option for one language, Left Option for the other. Switching costs a finger, not a menu.

  • Free and open source

    No trial, no word limit, no subscription. MIT licensed, with every line on GitHub.
